Welcome! I built this corner of my site as a place to write down things I run into while building software — mostly PHP, Node.js, React, and Vue, since that's where I spend most of my time.

Don't expect a strict schedule. I'll post here whenever I hit something worth writing down: a pattern that clicked, a bug that taught me something, or a tool that made my day better.

If you're a fellow engineer stopping by, I hope something here saves you a bit of time. Feel free to reach out — my contact details are on the [contact page](/contact).
